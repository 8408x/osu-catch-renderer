# Credits

The overlay styles here are recreations of community overlays from the tosu overlay collection
(https://github.com/tosuapp/counters, "a curated collection of community-made overlays available in the tosu dashboard").
Every design belongs to its author. Where an author is listed as "unknown", that is how the collection lists it.
Thanks to the tosu team (KotRik and Cherry) for tosu and for hosting the collection.

## Already used

| Part of this site | Overlay | Author |
|---|---|---|
| Map title, accuracy and combo (CPOL style) | AccuracyPP, ArtistTitleDifficulty, Combo ("SC_OverlayElements") | unknown |
| pp counter | InGame3 | Dartandr (https://github.com/Dartandr) |

The hit counts and progress bar widgets in the Custom style are drawn by this site, in simple styles of its own. The key presses widget keeps the site's own earlier key boxes.
