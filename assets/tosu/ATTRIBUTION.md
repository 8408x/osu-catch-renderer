`main.css` and the markup built in `src/tosu.js` come from the AccuracyPP, ArtistTitleDifficulty and Combo overlays
(v1.0.2, "SC_OverlayElements", author listed as "unknown") that ship in the static folder of tosu
(https://github.com/tosuapp/tosu). The three overlays share this exact stylesheet. The rendering is done by this site,
not by tosu.

`ingame3.css` and the pp counter markup in `src/tosu.js` come from InGame3 v1.004 by Dartandr (190x100, ingame / obs-overlay), also in tosu's static folder.
